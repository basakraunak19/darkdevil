Frequently asked questions

Why did my pairing code expire?

Pairing codes are temporary. Generate a new code and enter it in WhatsApp promptly.

Why is my phone number rejected?

Choose the correct country code, remove leading zeros, and enter only the digits of a number you own.

Is my pairing code safe to share?

No. Never share it with anyone. It can link your WhatsApp account to another device.

What should I do if the portal is offline?

Wait a moment and refresh the page. The live status panel will show when the service is available again.

Need help?

For pairing or deployment issues, contact the administrator of this DEVIL MD portal and include the error message shown on screen. Do not share your pairing code.
